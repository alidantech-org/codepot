# Review and handoff

A handoff must allow another contributor to continue without rediscovering the work.

Record:

- task ID and final status;
- commits or changed paths;
- exact commands and results;
- acceptance criteria confirmed or still open;
- architecture decisions and deviations;
- known risks or limitations;
- blockers and the next safe action.

Review the actual diff against allowed paths and non-goals. Do not rely only on test success. A passing suite does not prove that architecture, documentation, ownership, or scope rules were respected.
